Scopri

La Casa

Food from Naples
on Carrer de Muntaner

The mountain in our logo is Vesuvius, the volcano you can see from half of Naples. We put it there because that's where our recipes come from.

It's home cooking from the south of Italy: aubergine parmigiana, linguine with lobster, paccheri with truffle or a pizza straight out of the oven. We make the tiramisù every day.

We're in Sant Gervasi, between Diagonal and Via Augusta. Besides the main room there's a private room for groups and a small terrace on the pavement.

La Cucina

I nostri piatti

Six dishes from the menu, to give you an idea.

  1. I

    Linguine all'astice

    Lobster, clams and cherry tomatoes, and not much else. It doesn't need it.

  2. II

    Paccheri Pasquale

    It has Catalan sausage, seasonal mushrooms, mozzarella, parmesan, demi-glace and black truffle. Quite filling.

  3. III

    Parmigiana di melanzane

    Layers of aubergine, tomato and mozzarella, baked, with plenty of parmesan on top.

  4. IV

    Tagliata di vitello

    Half a kilo of sliced veal with demi-glace and roast potatoes. You can share it.

  5. V

    Pizza napoletana

    It comes out of the oven in the photo at the top of the page.

  6. VI

    Tiramisù della casa

    We make it every day, to the classic recipe. If you only order one dessert, make it this one.

Domande frequenti

Frequently asked questions

Where are you?

At Carrer de Muntaner, 328 (08021 Barcelona), in Sant Gervasi. Avinguda Diagonal is a few minutes' walk away.

What kind of food do you serve?

Italian, mostly Neapolitan: pasta, pizza from the oven, some meat and desserts. We make the tiramisù every day.

What are your opening hours?

Tuesday and Sunday, 1:30 to 4 pm. Wednesday to Saturday, 1:30 to 4 pm and 8:30 to 11:30 pm. We're closed on Mondays.

How do I book?

Use the booking button on this page or call us on +34 933 03 44 82.

Do you have vegetarian dishes?

Yes, a few. If you have any allergies or intolerances, tell us when you book or when you order.

Can I book a private event?

Yes. We have a private room for groups, birthdays and work lunches. Call us and we'll set it up.

Do you have a terrace?

Yes, on the pavement of Carrer de Muntaner.
